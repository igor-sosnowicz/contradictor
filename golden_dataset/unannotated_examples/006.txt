In order to get homelessness to decrease dramatically, the government should build more shelters for the homeless and teach the public about the real cause of homelessness. When I was about one or two, I lived in a homeless shelter with my mom and my two older brothers. Thinking back on that experience, I wonder about the other kids that I was friends with. Where are they now? Are they still living in shelters? Are they living in a home of their own. Many families are in the same place my family was. They might never get the chance to know what it is like to have a home of their own and the sense of security a home provides.
