This is especially important because if students don't participate in community service it can affect them in college, networking, or even finding a job opportunity. As Dorothy I. Height put it, without community service "we wouldn't have a strong quality of life – be able to grow and develop." (Caldwell) Furthermore, "Students who volunteered had a 27% better chance of finding a job than those who didn't." (Hientz) The consequences of not participating in community service are clear.
