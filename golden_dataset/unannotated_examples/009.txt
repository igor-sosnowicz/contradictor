Philosophers of science often say that in science we don’t prove anything with 100% certainty but that we can disprove hypotheses with 100% certainty. Consequently, scientific concepts must have the possibility of being falsified. Good scientists spend much time trying to disprove their ideas. When various tests fail to disprove those ideas, the more confidence we have that our ideas are true. Flat-earthers seem to understand at least part of this approach, which is why they latched onto this photograph, calling it “the black swan,” thinking that it disproves the earth is a globe. Of course, they are wrong about that.
