What should I look for in a mechanical keyboard?

Started by keyboard_newbie · September 11, 2026

keyboard_newbie New member

I'm buying my first mechanical keyboard and I'm having trouble understanding what actually matters. I mostly write documents and occasionally play games.

Should I focus on the type of switch, the layout, or the quality of the keycaps? My budget is around $120.

quiet_typist Member

For your use case I would start with the switches. If you work around other people, consider quieter linear or tactile switches. Switch preference is subjective, so trying a few types before buying is useful.

layout_fan Member

Don't underestimate layout. A compact board can save desk space, but removing dedicated navigation keys may be annoying if you write a lot. A standard tenkeyless layout is a good compromise for many people.

old_timer Moderator

Keycap quality matters mostly for feel and long-term wear. At this price point I would prioritize a solid board, hot-swappable switches, and a layout you are comfortable using every day.
