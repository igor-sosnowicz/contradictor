What I Learned From a Month Without Notifications

September 10, 2026 · Alex Turner

A month ago I turned off almost every notification on my phone. I expected the experiment to make me more productive, but the biggest change was surprisingly simple: I stopped feeling that I was constantly behind.

The first few days were strange

During the first week I repeatedly unlocked my phone without knowing why. The habit was so automatic that I sometimes checked the screen before remembering that there was nothing new to see.

After several days, the behavior became less frequent. I began checking messages at specific times rather than responding whenever a small notification appeared.

More uninterrupted time

The most useful effect was not that I spent dramatically less time online. Instead, the time I spent working became less fragmented. Reading a document for twenty minutes without interruption felt much easier than it had before.

The goal was never to use my phone less. It was to let me decide when the phone deserved my attention.

Would I do it again?

Yes. I still keep notifications enabled for calls and a few genuinely important services, but most applications remain silent. I now check them when I choose rather than when they choose for me.

It is a small change, but it has made my days feel considerably calmer.
