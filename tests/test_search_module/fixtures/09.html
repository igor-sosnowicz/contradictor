Global Energy Report: What Changed This Year?

Energy markets continued to change rapidly during the past year, with renewable generation increasing while electricity demand grew in several major economies.

Key findings

  • Global electricity demand increased compared with the previous year.
  • Solar generation recorded another year of strong growth.
  • Battery storage capacity expanded in several major markets.
  • Natural gas remained an important source of flexible generation.

Regional differences

Growth was not evenly distributed. Some regions added substantial new renewable capacity, while others continued to rely heavily on existing thermal power plants.

  1. North America increased utility-scale battery deployment.
  2. Europe expanded offshore wind capacity.
  3. Asia accounted for a large share of new solar installations.
  4. Several emerging markets focused on grid reliability.

Looking ahead

The next stage of the transition will depend not only on adding generation capacity but also on modernizing transmission networks, improving storage, and managing periods of high demand.

The report concludes that energy systems are becoming more diverse, but infrastructure investment will remain essential to maintaining reliable electricity supplies.
