Local Observatory Opens New Research Center

The North Valley Observatory has opened a new research facility dedicated to studying distant galaxies and transient astronomical events.

September 12, 2026 · 08:45 AM · By Sarah Bennett

The North Valley Observatory officially opened its new research center on Saturday, bringing together astronomers from six universities and several independent research groups.

The facility contains a data center, laboratories, and offices for visiting scientists. Its primary purpose is to process observations collected by the observatory's new wide-field telescope.

A larger view of the sky

The telescope is designed to repeatedly photograph large sections of the night sky. Researchers hope this will allow them to identify objects that change brightness or position over relatively short periods.

“The important thing is not just taking one picture,” said observatory director Helen Brooks. “We want to understand how the sky changes.”

International collaboration

Data from the center will be shared with research teams in several countries. Observatory officials said the project is intended to make astronomical data available to researchers who do not have access to large telescopes.

The first public research results are expected to be released early next year.
