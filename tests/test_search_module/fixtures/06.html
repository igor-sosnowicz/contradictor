Solar energy

Solar energy is energy derived from sunlight. It can be converted into electricity or used directly as heat. Solar energy is considered a renewable resource because sunlight is continually supplied by the Sun.

Solar energy

Type: Renewable energy

Primary source: Sunlight

Common technologies: Photovoltaics, solar thermal

History

Humans have used sunlight for heating and illumination for thousands of years. Modern solar technology developed through advances in materials science, electrical engineering, and manufacturing.

Technology

Photovoltaic cells convert light directly into electrical energy. Solar thermal systems instead capture heat from sunlight for uses such as hot water production or electricity generation.

Common solar technologies
Technology Primary output Typical use
Photovoltaic Electricity Buildings and solar farms
Solar thermal Heat Water heating
Concentrated solar Heat and electricity Large power plants

Applications

  • Residential electricity generation
  • Commercial power generation
  • Water heating
  • Remote telecommunications equipment
  • Spacecraft power systems

Advantages

Solar systems can generate electricity without directly burning fuel. They can also be installed at many different scales, from small residential systems to large utility installations.

Limitations

Solar generation varies with weather, season, and time of day. Large installations may also require significant amounts of land and electrical infrastructure.
