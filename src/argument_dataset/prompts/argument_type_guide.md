- **contrargument**: attacks the underlying reasoning, premises, or inference.
Logic: "Target's premises do not support their conclusion."
- **counterexample**: provides a specific instance invalidating a general claim.
Logic: "Target claims 'All X are Y', but here is an X that is not Y."
- **contradiction**: asserts a statement that directly conflicts with the target claim, without new evidence.
Logic: "Target claims A, but B is asserted, where A and B cannot both be true."
- **rebuttal**: refutes the target's conclusion with opposing evidence. Logic: "Target claims X, but evidence E proves non-X."
